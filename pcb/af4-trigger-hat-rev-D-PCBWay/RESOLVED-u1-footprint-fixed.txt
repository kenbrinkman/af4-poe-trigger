RESOLVED 2026-08-28 - this package is current and valid.

U1 now uses a PROJECT-LOCAL footprint authored from Panasonic's own
"Recommended mounting pad (TOP VIEW)" drawing in semi_eng_gu_sop4_1a.pdf:

    footprints/aF4.pretty/AQY212GS_SOP4_Panasonic.kicad_mod

    pad size ............ 0.8 mm across the pitch axis
                          1.2 mm along the lead axis
    pad centres ......... 2.54 mm apart on the pitch axis
                          6.00 mm apart across the span
    tolerance ........... +/-0.1 mm

Pads span 2.40..3.60 mm from the part centre. The lead tip sits at
3.2..3.6 mm (6.8 +/-0.4 lead span), so the toe lands on the pad across the
whole tolerance range.

TWO EARLIER FOOTPRINTS WERE WRONG. Recorded because the failure mode repeats:
  1. SO-4_4.4x2.3mm_P1.27mm - 1.27 mm pitch, an OPTEK OPIA403 pattern.
     Every pin missed its pad by 0.635 mm. Chosen because the NAME matched.
  2. SO-4_4.4x4.3mm_P2.54mm - correct pitch, correct body, correct pad
     CENTRES, but the pads are rotated 90 degrees: 0.8 mm along the lead axis
     where Panasonic asks for 1.2. Would have mounted, with no toe fillet at
     the far end of the lead-span tolerance. Chosen by matching DIMENSIONS,
     which is better than matching a name, and still not the same as reading
     the recommended-pad drawing.
